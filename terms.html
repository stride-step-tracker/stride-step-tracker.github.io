What Stride provides

Stride helps you track daily steps, set goals, explore your history and earn achievements. Google Drive backup is optional. You do not need a Google connection to use local tracking.

Step counts and wellbeing

Step counts are estimates based on your device's sensors. Results can vary with your phone, how you carry it, permissions, battery settings and non-walking movement. Tracking can be interrupted or delayed. Goals, achievements and reports are for personal activity awareness; Stride is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Choose activity goals appropriate to your circumstances.

Your records and backups

You retain your rights in your records. Local progress is stored on your device. Clearing app storage, uninstalling, losing your device or replacing records during an import or restore may remove local progress. Keep an independent export if you need an additional recovery copy.

If you enable Google Drive backup, you authorise Stride to store and retrieve its backup files in the selected account's private application-data folder. Backups depend on Google services, account access, available storage, connectivity and Android scheduling. An automatic interval is not a guaranteed upload time. Check the last successful backup before relying on it.

Restore only a copy you intend to use: a confirmed restore replaces saved progress. Recovery copies are limited and should not be treated as permanent archives. Disconnecting Drive leaves cloud copies in place. Removing Stride's hidden application data through Google Drive deletes those copies. Further details are in the privacy policy.
