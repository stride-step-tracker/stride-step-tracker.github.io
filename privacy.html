YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy covers the Stride Android step-tracking app and this information website. Stride is maintained by Jake Woods. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to jake.13.5.2016@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Stride uses your phone's activity sensors and activity-recognition permission to count steps. It stores daily step totals and dates, your daily goal, achievement identifiers and achievement dates, and preferences such as tracking pauses and backup settings. These records support goals, history, trends, achievements, notifications and the widget. Stride does not request GPS location to count your steps.

Optional Google Drive backup

You can use Stride without connecting Google. If you connect a Google account, Stride stores the selected account email and connection settings locally to manage backups. Google handles account authorisation; Stride does not receive your Google password or store an access token persistently.

With your permission, Stride reads and writes backup files in its private Google Drive application-data folder, using the drive.appdata permission. This does not grant access to your general Drive files. Backups contain your daily step records and update timestamps, daily goal, and achievement identifiers and dates, with backup timestamps and integrity information. An installation identifier helps manage recovery copies.

You choose manual backup or an automatic schedule. Automatic backups run when Android permits and the selected connection conditions are met. Restoring downloads the chosen copy and replaces progress only after your confirmation. Google processes and stores cloud data under its privacy policy.

How information is used and shared

Stride uses Google user data only to provide and manage the backup and restore feature you choose. It does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or send your step history to a developer-operated server. The current app contains no advertising or analytics integration. Data you export or share manually goes to the destination you select.

Stride's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. It is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you replace or remove it, clear Android app storage, or uninstall the app. Keep an export or verified backup before doing so. Stride retains up to ten verified normal Drive recovery copies per installation through its backup cleanup process; copies from other installations are not automatically removed by that installation. Failed cleanup may leave additional copies. Local safety copies created before restoring are limited to five.

Disconnecting Google Drive in Stride stops its automatic Drive backups but keeps existing cloud copies. You can revoke permission in your Google Account connections. Neither action deletes cloud backups. To remove those copies, use Google Drive's settings for managing Stride's hidden application data. This deletes the stored Drive backups and prevents restoring from those deleted copies. Exported files must be deleted separately wherever you saved or shared them. Android or device backup services may also retain data according to your device settings.

Security

Stride connects to Google Drive over HTTPS and checks backup integrity before accepting a backup. Backup files are not separately password-encrypted by Stride. Device and Google account security remain important; an integrity check is not encryption.

This website

These pages use no analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process visitor technical information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link subjects your visit to that provider's policies.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Stride's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. For questions, contact jake.13.5.2016@gmail.com. The developer cannot directly access or delete the private backup files in your Google account.
